Optics Theory
Apparent birefringence: δ' = |nε' - nω|
Difference between the two allowed refractive indices on the cut.
Notice the difference between apparent and maximum birefringence.
Retardation: Γ = d × δ' × 1000
Path difference (nm) = Thickness (µm) × Birefringence × 1000.
Path difference accumulated as the slow ray lags behind the fast ray through thickness d.
Determines interference color.
Fast vs. Slow Rays:
Light splits into two rays vibrating 90° apart.
Lower n = Fast Ray (Velocity is higher)
Higher n = Slow Ray (Velocity is lower)
Accessory Plate Rule:
If the Slow Ray vibration direction of the mineral aligns with the Slow Ray of the gypsum plate, retardations add (interference colors shift up). If they cross, they subtract (shift down).
